## Projects

### Heartbeat or Noise? *(In Progress)*
Training ML classifiers on 8,500+ clinical ECG recordings to test whether a consumer wearable's signals can serve as a low-barrier cardiac screening proxy. Cardiovascular disease causes 30.5% of all deaths in Singapore — yet 49% of residents haven't had a health checkup in the past year.

Built with Python, scikit-learn, and Pandas.

[Source code](https://github.com/Lucas-sam93/ga-capstone-heartbeat-or-noise)

### W.O.W. — Worth-Or-What?
An HDB resale price estimator achieving 95.13% R² accuracy across 270,620 transactions in 26 towns, with a town recommendation engine reaching 99.71% accuracy.

Built with Python, LightGBM, and Flask.

### JD-Match
A resume optimization tool powered by Google's Gemini AI. Scores resume-to-job alignment across 15 categories, flags missing keywords, and generates rewritten bullet points you can apply with one click.

Built with React, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, and the Gemini API.

[Live app](https://jd-match.vercel.app) · [Source code](https://github.com/Lucas-sam93/JD-Match)

### Singapore Parliamentary Elections 1955–2025
A 6-dashboard Tableau story tracking 70 years of elections. The Workers' Party wins more than half the votes in every seat they contest — yet holds just 9.3% of parliament. Built to show why the quiet revolution is already happening, hidden by a structural bottleneck.

Built with Tableau.

[Live dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/lucas.sam4725/viz/SingaporeParliamentaryElections19552025/D1-TheParadox) · [Source code](https://github.com/Lucas-sam93/SG-Elections-Tableau)

### Gender Pay Gap Analysis
Queried 10,000+ UK companies in BigQuery and found that women's representation drops 13 percentage points from entry-level to senior roles — a 'glass ceiling' effect worst in Finance and nearly absent in Care. Built an interactive dashboard to let users explore the data by sector, year, and company size.

Built with SQL, Python, Plotly, and Pandas.

[Live dashboard](https://lucas-sam93.github.io/gender-pay-gap-analysis/) · [Source code](https://github.com/Lucas-sam93/gender-pay-gap-analysis)
